# PM2.5, European air quality data for 2024, (interpolated data), interim data, June 2025

The data set provides values for the human health related indicator of PM2.5 (annual average concentration) at 1 km2 grid combining up-to-date monitoring air quality data, chemical transport model results and other supplementary data in a ‘regression-interpolation-merging mapping’ methodology. It covers Europe for the year 2025.

This data set is used to estimate population exposure to air pollution in 2025 for the EEA´s European Air quality city viewer. The methodology can be found in the 2025 ETC HE report 2025/4 "Interim air quality maps of EEA member and cooperating countries for 2024. PM, O3, and NO2 spatial estimates.", available at https://www.eionet.europa.eu/etcs/etc-he/products/etc-he-products/etc-he-reports/etc-he-report-2025-4-interim-air-quality-maps-of-eea-member-and-cooperating-countries-for-2024-pm-o3-and-no2-spatial-estimates (the corresponding 2026 report with the 2025 maps will be published at the end of 2026).
