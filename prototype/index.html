Europe’s air got cleaner. But not everywhere equally.

A first interactive prototype using European Environment Agency PM2.5 raster data. The story guides readers from long-term improvement to the regions where pollution remains persistent.

Setup

Europe-wide PM2.5 overview

The story begins with a Europe-wide view of PM2.5 in 2007 and 2024. The raster files do not cover exactly the same area, but they introduce the broad pattern: PM2.5 decreased strongly, while remaining pollution is still spatially uneven.

Narrative role: Setup. Pattern: Familiarization and Concretize.
Prototype status: early interactive structure. Static analysis outputs are connected to story stages.
