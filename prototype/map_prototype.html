What is PM2.5?

PM2.5 means fine particulate matter with a diameter smaller than 2.5 micrometers. These particles are small enough to enter deep into the lungs and are linked to serious health risks.

Start here: the story first explains why PM2.5 matters, then shows that Europe improved overall, before focusing on the hotspot regions that still remain.
